Shuqi Sheng was a Physics PhD student at the Institute of High Energy Physics (IHEP) in Beijing, China, working on the LHCb experiment.  I had the privilege to co-supervise Shuqi's doctoral research from November 2021 to June 2025, beginning during my time as a Chung-Yao Chao Fellow at IHEP.

## Activities during doctoral research

Shuqi was able to learn and apply significant HEP detector and computing skills during the doctoral project:

1. Installation and commissioning of the LHCb Upstream Tracker for the Run 3 upgrade
2. Development and validation of the LHCb Stripping Project, reprocessing the full 2016-2018 LHCb data set with legacy software
3. Large-scale data processing and production on distributed computing resources
4. Co-authorship of [The LHCb Stripping Project: Sustainable Legacy Data Processing for High-Energy Physics](/publications/2025-stripping/), published in Computing and Software for Big Science

## Current Activity

Shuqi is currently a postdoctoral researcher at EPFL in Lausanne, Switzerland.
