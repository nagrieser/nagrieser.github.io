Gabriel Nowak is a Physics PhD student at the University of Cincinnati working on the LHCb experiment.  I have had the privilege to co-supervise Gabriel's doctoral research since July 2022.

## Activities during doctoral research

Gabriel has been able to learn and apply significant HEP analysis skills during the doctoral project:

1. Measurement of the $WW$ production cross-section in the forward region at LHCb
2. Large-scale data processing and selection of diboson events in LHCb data
3. Monte-Carlo simulation and modelling of electroweak diboson production
4. Application of statistical inference tools to extract the cross-section and its uncertainties

## Current Activity

Gabriel is currently a PhD candidate at the University of Cincinnati, continuing the $WW$ cross-section measurement.
