I design research projects that give students real ownership of a physics question, from the first simulation to a public report and presentation. The students listed here worked with me through the programs below; their positions are funded by those programs and by their home institutions.

- **CERN Summer Student Programme.** I have designed and supervised a project every summer since 2023. Undergraduates apply directly to [CERN](https://careers.cern/summer), and selected students are matched with supervisors.
- **Undergraduate capstone and thesis projects.** A summer project can continue as a capstone or senior thesis with your home department.
- **Post-baccalaureate research.** Graduates continuing a project before graduate school.
- **Doctoral research.** I co-supervise the research of PhD students on LHCb at the institutions where I work.
