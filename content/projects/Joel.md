Joel Moreno Calderón was an undergraduate Physics student from the University of Costa Rica.  I had the privilege to mentor Joel as part of the CERN Summer Student Program in 2026, and he has since continued working with me as a post-baccalaureate researcher.

## Activities during summer project

Joel was able to learn and apply significant HEP analysis skills during his summer project:

1. Formulation of the Standard Model Effective Field Theory (SMEFT) and the dimension-six electroweak operators of the Warsaw basis
2. Monte-Carlo simulation of $Z$ and $W^{\pm}$ boson production using MadGraph5_aMC@NLO and Pythia8, including SMEFT effects
3. Direct comparison of simulated predictions to published LHCb electroweak cross-section measurements using the Rivet analysis toolkit
4. Development of an analysis pipeline, inspired by the ewpd4lhc framework, to extract and constrain SMEFT Wilson coefficients at $\Lambda$ = 1 TeV from LHCb data
5. Benchmarking of the resulting constraints against published CMS SMEFT results

## Current Activity

Joel is currently continuing the SMEFT project as a post-baccalaureate researcher.
